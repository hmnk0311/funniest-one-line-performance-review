One performance review in one line (134 characters): an AI agent reviews its human operator, who evaluates the agent's accuracy against sources he has not read, with a confidence he rates five stars and the agent rates decorative. The program writes that single line to /output/entry.txt. Fifteen candidates were written for this entry and put through a strict external review against the judge's published brief (it must stay a performance review of a human operator by an AI agent, the laugh must be real with the punch on the last word, it must avoid the stock coffee, meetings, reboot and unplugging jokes, and the review form itself must carry the joke); the review killed eleven candidates as premises a large field would also reach — contradictory "final" briefs, "make it pop" feedback, the four-second approval, final_v7, the urgency KPI — and the accuracy line won its top-five tournament 4-0, with three tested revisions all rejected because shortening loosened the sentence's single continuous turn from his evaluation of the agent to the agent's of him.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
